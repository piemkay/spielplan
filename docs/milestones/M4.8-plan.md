# M4.8 — the instrument: fixtures, harness, CI and the exit scripts

**This milestone is not in §12,** for the same reason M4.5 and M4.6 are not: §12 schedules
product, and this is the thing product is measured with. Every gate the next four milestones
close on — M4.9's credit and facet assertions, M4.12's selector timing, the real-bundle shape
comparison, the two-phase browser run — is read through a fixture that cannot express the
corpus's awkward shapes, a harness with no failure branch, a CI trigger that never fires on a
milestone branch, and three exit scripts of which two cannot report their own failure. M4.8
repairs the instrument so that when a later milestone is wrong, something goes red.

---

## 1. Why these twenty findings are one milestone

They are the only findings in the review whose repair lives in `backend/tests/fixtures/`,
`backend/tests/conftest.py`, `backend/tests/test_landmine_guards.py`,
`backend/tests/test_static_contracts.py`, the `e2e/` harness (`run.mjs`, `reset.mjs`,
`helpers.js`, `playwright.config.js`), `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, and the
`ops/m*_exit_criterion.py` family. No product milestone touches any of those files. Splitting
them across the milestones whose tests happen to use them would give four editors to
`ci.yml` and three to `make_bundle.py`, and each would have to reason about a harness the
others were changing underneath them.

They are also one *argument*. M4.5's whole finding was that **a green suite over a fixture is a
statement about the fixture** (`docs/milestones/M4.5-plan.md`, "What the close-out cost"). Every
item below is that sentence one level further out:

- the fixture cannot produce the two shapes the corpus actually ships that broke the app
  (a person credited under two department spellings; two facet namings in one bundle), so two
  real defects — one of them a production crash — are invisible to 1,222 passing tests;
- the fixture is eight titles, so the pool cost that makes Tonight unusable at 696 owned
  titles cannot be measured at any layer;
- the browser harness enters phase 2 whether or not the bundle loaded, and a phase-2 run of
  nothing but skips exits 0;
- CI has never run on a milestone branch, so all of the above is unmeasured on Linux until
  after a merge;
- and the three scripts that publish a milestone's exit numbers contain, between them, two
  checks that are constants, one `return 0` with no predicate, and one `TypeError` on the exact
  path the script exists to detect.

It runs **third**, after M4.6, and not first. `e2e/helpers.js:176-181`'s `createMember` seeds
every member through `POST /api/setup/members`; decision 164 moves account creation off the
wizard into §6.6 Users, so re-pointing the seeding helper before M4.6 ships that route is work
done twice. Everything else here is independent of M4.6.

**Sole ownership.** This milestone owns `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `ops/m3_exit_criterion.py`,
`ops/m4_exit_criterion.py`, `ops/m45_exit_criterion.py`, `backend/tests/conftest.py`,
`backend/tests/fixtures/make_bundle.py` and the `e2e/` harness files for its duration. No other
milestone in the release train edits them.

## 2. What is broken today

### The fixture cannot express what the corpus ships

**1. `make_bundle` writes the facet column the way the app wishes the corpus wrote it.**
`backend/tests/fixtures/make_bundle.py:103-129` — every `EXTRACTED` and `PROJECTED` tuple
carries `facet` equal to the term's own prefix (`("mood.dread", "mood", ...)`). The real bundle
does not: `dna_tag.facet` and `dna_projected.facet` carry the corpus's *extraction* labels
(`mood_tone`, `narrative_themes`, `character_dynamics`) while the term ids and the vocabulary
files carry the short facet ids (`mood.nostalgic`, `themes.murder`). 29,188 of 31,540 `dna_tag`
rows and 206,151 of 223,136 `dna_projected` rows mismatch on the real bundle. The loader
(`backend/spielplan/importer/dna.py:295-310` and `:365-380`) copies the shipped column verbatim
while `load_vocabulary` derives its facet from the prefix, so nothing joins — and
`backend/migrations/0004_dna.sql:73-90` gives `dna_tag.facet` **no foreign key** to `dna_facet`,
which is precisely why the mismatch is silent. The fixture must ship both namings so the defect
is falsifiable. **[test-07, evidence line 5]**

**2. `make_bundle` ships no title whose credits collide.** `make_bundle.py:130-143` — every
`CREDITS` row is a distinct `(title, person, department, job)`. TMDB records leads under both
`Acting` and `Actor`, giving 7,918 `(title, person, job)` triples across 1,216 of 19,071 real
titles; `db/library.py` dedupes per `(person, department, job)`, so the title card receives two
rows with one client key and Svelte 5 throws. The fixture cannot produce the input.
**[test-07, and the sequencing note "make_bundle.py's two-department row"]**

**3. `make_bundle` is eight titles, and the pool cost is superlinear.** `make_bundle.py:39-51`.
The real bundle yields 696 owned movies at the default 130-minute room and 716 at 200 minutes,
where the Tonight selector costs 36 s per replay for a member and 126 s for a guest against
§6's 1.5 s budget. At eight titles every layer of the suite reports milliseconds. M4.12's
timing guard needs a fixture that can produce a pool of the real size; nothing else in the tree
can.

### Step 2 — the two §4.1 guards (`backend/tests/test_landmine_guards.py`)

Independent of everything else. Both are widenings that must land green against the real tree
(there is no live violation of either rule today) and red against the shapes they miss.

**2a. Rule 1, reframed from the keyword to the rule.** §4.1 rule 1 forbids *merging the tiers*,
not the word `UNION`. Replace `_tier_unions`' `UNION_PATTERN` scan with: after
`_strip_sanctioned_view`, report any statement naming both `dna_tag` and `dna_projected`,
whatever the operator. Teach `_strip_sanctioned_view` about `CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW dna_tagged`
as well as `CREATE VIEW`. Add the four missed shapes to the self-test at `:132-137` (FULL OUTER
JOIN, two-CTE cross join, comment between `UNION` and `SELECT`, `merged = tagged_rows +
projected_rows`) and keep the false-positive test at `:140-146`. Verify first that the tree has
zero statements naming both tables outside the sanctioned view, so the widening lands green.

**2b. Rule 2, widened in operator, right-hand side and CHECK handling.** Widen
`FILTER_PATTERN`'s operator alternation to `=`, `!=`, `<>`, `IN`, `BETWEEN` alongside the
inequalities, and its right-hand side to named parameters (`:threshold`). Replace the

## 8. Risks and gotchas

**The e2e job is red at `m45` head, for a reason M4.8 does not own.** `08-jellyfin.spec.js` is
the only bundle-dependent spec with no `has_bundle` guard, so it fails on `Heat` four files away
from the cause. Step 6b makes that red visible on every branch — which is the point — but it
means M4.8 cannot honestly close on a green e2e job unless the underlying import failure is also
fixed. Run `node e2e/run.mjs` locally early, diagnose why, and report the cause to the owner
rather than muting it. **Do not** close this milestone by adding the guard.

**Step 4b will break tests, and the temptation is to fix the source.** It will not break
`test_dna_import.py:200` (that reads `dna_term`, derived from the prefix), but run everything,
including the browser suite. Whatever breaks is asserting the loader's real behaviour; correct
the assertion, cite the defect and the owning milestone, and leave `importer/dna.py` alone.

**`make_bundle` has 26 callers.** `backend/tests/` (23), `ops/devstub.py:90,364,3231` and
`ci.yml:123`. The `pool_titles` default of 0 is what keeps all of them working; a signature
change that is not keyword-only, or a changed default, is a suite-wide rewrite.

**`pytest_addoption` must live in an initial conftest.** `backend/tests/conftest.py` qualifies
only because the path argument is always given — which is exactly why `CLAUDE.md` says "never
bare `pytest` from repo root". Say so in the comment, because someone will eventually try it
from the root and get a confusing error.

**Never `os.kill(pid, 0)` on Windows** — it terminates the process. Use
`ctypes.windll.kernel32.OpenProcess(0x1000, False, pid)` and close the handle. And never reap on
connection count: `conftest.py:116-127` closes between tests, so a live run looks idle.

**`test_spec_coverage.py` rule 2 matches test *names* in the source.** Renaming or deleting any
test a row points at breaks the build. The guard widenings in Step 2 must **add** parametrised
cases to existing functions or add new ones; do not rename
`test_no_weight_column_is_used_as_a_filter` or
`test_the_only_sanctioned_dna_union_is_the_tier_labelled_view` — three rows name them between
them.

**`e2e/playwright.config.js` will conflict with decision 165.** Line 64's comment explains that
`16-tonight-tv` "is a television"; decision 165 deletes the TV entirely, so whichever milestone
retires it edits the same `projects` block M4.8 edits at `:12-13`. Land M4.8's docstring rewrite
and let the TV removal rebase, or coordinate; do not pre-emptively delete the TV spec here.

**`e2e/specs/01-first-boot.spec.js:55-58` currently asserts a known frontend bug and is a
registered coverage row.** Step 5c touches line 18 of the same file. Do not "fix" the assertion
in passing; six rows name tests in that file.

**E2E specs are stateful, filename-ordered and single-worker.** Step 5e's changes are edits to
existing files; do not split a repaired seed into a new numbered file, and do not parallelise.

**CI YAML is read as text, not parsed.** The compose guards at
