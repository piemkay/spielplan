# M5.7 — Admin · Connectors and System: the providers, the spend guard, and what the box is doing

> **This is a proposal, not an amendment.** Decisions **324, 325, 330** and **339, 343** are recorded
> in [`ROADMAP-M5.md`](ROADMAP-M5.md#decisions-the-owner-must-take-numbered-from-321). 324 forbids a
> test until it is taken; 330 blocks this milestone's gate. Only **M5.1** edits the spec file.

§6.6's spend guard is the only surface in this app where a household can be surprised by a bill:

> "Spend guard: **per-title cost estimate before enabling**, monthly cap, running meter reading
> '$4.12 of $25.00 this month' (corpus baseline: ~$0.005-0.01/title/pass on Gemini batch)."
> (`docs/spielplan-spec_v2.1.md:321`)

"Before enabling" is the whole requirement, and it is a sequencing claim about a form: the number
appears **before** the setting persists. That is the thesis of this milestone, and everything else on
these two pages — the three provider cards, the source-connector cards, Jellyfin's webhook status and
library pick, and §6.6's System remainder — is the rest of the two cards that promise it.

It runs last, in parallel with M5.6, for the M4.15 reason: the shell restyles surfaces only after the
backends behind them stop moving.

---

## 1. Why these are one milestone

**Two pages, both owned here, and neither shared with anyone.** `admin/connectors/+page.svelte` (530
lines) and `admin/system/+page.svelte` (242 lines) are this milestone's hotspots. The file-locality
test gave **all** frontend admin files to M5.6 and M5.7 precisely so that M5.2 (which wants the
webhook status) and M5.5 (which wants the provider cards) do not both open the Connectors page.

**Queue depth belongs with the providers.** §6.6's System list is five things and three shipped at
M4.7 (decision 182). The two that did not — queue depth, last syncs — are the acquisition pipeline's
own numbers, so the milestone that renders the spend meter for that pipeline is the one that should
render how much work it has.

**One estimate function, two callers.** M5.5 exports the per-title cost; M5.6's flywheel multiplies
it by a selection and M5.7's provider card multiplies it by one title. Splitting them across three
milestones is how two estimates come to disagree.

**Depends on:** M5.1 (queue depth), M5.2 (webhook status, library list), M5.5 (providers, prices,
meter). **Parallel with:** M5.6.

## 3. Owner decisions this milestone cannot take alone

**Decision 324 — parallel mode's default and the default pass count.** Blocks the toggle's initial
state and, by `spec_coverage.toml:3159`, blocks any test of it. Recommended: off, one pass.

**Decision 325 — the cap's period and default.** Blocks the meter's caption ("this month" has to mean
something) and the cap input's validation. Recommended: calendar month in the install's `TZ`, and a
default the owner names — the arithmetic in
[`ROADMAP-M5.md`](ROADMAP-M5.md#what-the-paid-stage-costs-and-what-the-cap-actually-gates) puts the
unattended path at ~$1.30/month, so any default above about $5 never binds §12's criterion and the
cap exists for the flywheel's Launch button.

**Decision 330 — proposals 104, 107, 109, 135.** 107 is this milestone's (the spend cap's editable
half). Its coverage row `map-taste-admin-spend-cap-parks-paid-stage` (`:3171`) currently cites a
clean `spec` string, but `jellyfin-acquisition-eval-spend-cap-meters-billed-tokens` (`:3207`, M5.5's)
cites proposals 107 and 109 and cannot go green until the numbers are settled.

**Decision 339 — does per-task assignment ship with all three tasks or with extraction alone?**
Recommended: extraction alone. "Query parsing" is §6.4's compositional search (M6) and "conflict
phrasing" is §6.2/§6.5 copy; `ROADMAP-to-M5.md:578` says the conflict-phrasing task *"has no caller
for the same reason"*. A settings control for a task nothing calls is a promise the surface cannot
keep.

**Decision 343 — where model prices live and what happens when they go stale.** Evidence in §2.7.
Recommended: a shipped table **with a `valid_until`**, an admin override, a refusal to guess for an
unknown model (`mdc/config.py:190-200`'s `price_for` explains why a bare `startswith` is how a cost
estimate invents a number), and a caption that says which price and which date the estimate used.

## 4. The work, in order

Phase A is the one clause the milestone is named for. Phases B-D are the rest of Connectors. Phase E
is System and it trips the budgeted red diff.

### Phase A — the spend guard

**A1. `SpendMeter.svelte`** — the cap input, the running meter, and the remaining figure. The meter
is a read of M5.5's `SUM` over `llm_call`, never a counter.

**A2. "Before enabling" is a sequence, and the test is about the sequence.** Enabling a provider, or
turning parallel mode on, **returns the per-title estimate and the projected monthly figure against
the remaining cap before the setting is persisted**. Implement it as an explicit preview call rather
than as an optimistic save plus a rollback; the coverage row's `what` is about ordering
(`spec_coverage.toml:3157`).

**A3. The projected monthly figure needs a rate.** "Per-title x titles per month" needs a titles-per-
month number; the honest source is the install's own recent acquisition rate rather than an invented
constant. If there is no history, say so rather than printing a guess.

**A4. The caption carries the thinking-token note.** §9's 5x undercount is invisible to an operator
anywhere else, and proposal 107 argues the caption is the only place it surfaces. Whether §6.6 gains
the clause is decision 330; the caption should carry it either way, because it is true.

**A5. The caption names the price and its date**, per decision 343. A meter reading "$4.12 of $25.00"
against a price that doubles on a known date should say which price it used.

**A6. At the cap, the surface says what happens.** Stage 6 parks `over spend cap` (M5.5's behaviour);
this card names it, and an admin retry that would breach is refused with that reason shown.

### Phase B — the LLM provider cards

**B1. `LlmProviderCard.svelte`**, three instances. Key (write-only, masked after save — the Jellyfin
card's existing idiom at `admin/connectors/+page.svelte:10-12`: *"the field posts empty to mean 'keep
the stored one' and the page can only tell you whether there is a key"*), model pick, test button.

**B2. Each card captions its structured-output mode** — Gemini "responseSchema", Anthropic "forced
tool-use", OpenAI "strict schema". §6.6 names all three (`spec:321`).

**B3. The un-configured state is a first-class state.** Proposal 108 (`spec-v2.2-proposals.md:1301`)
calls the dashed, dimmed "no key" card normative. Whether §6.6 gains that sentence is decision 330's
business; a visibly un-configured card is right regardless.

**B4. Per-task assignment**, per decision 339. If extraction alone: one control, and the other two
task names do not appear.

**B5. Parallel mode**, per decision 324. **No test may assert its default** until that decision
lands, so the component's initial value comes from the server and the test asserts the round trip
rather than the value.

**B6. The batch/sync toggle**, per **decision 338** (M5.5's). If batch does not ship at M5, the
toggle ships disabled with its reason or does not ship at all — not enabled and inert.

### Phase C — the source connector cards

**C1. `SourceConnectorCard.svelte`** for TMDB, OMDb and Trakt, over the seeds
`registry.py:88-99` already writes and M5.5's generic `ConnectorSpec`. Key, test button, and the
same write-only masking.

**C2. Which stage-2 sources need credentials is worth saying on the card.** Three of §8 stage 2's
eight do; the other five are keyless, and proposal 137 (`spec-v2.2-proposals.md:1627`) records the
idea so that *"acquisition failures point at the right admin card"*.

**C3. Delete `setup/+page.svelte:138-139`'s two placeholders** once the cards exist — the wizard's
"configure in Admin · M5" lines become links.

### Phase D — the Jellyfin card's two missing halves

**D1. Library pick** — a multi-select over M5.2's `/Library/MediaFolders` read, writing
`JellyfinConfig.library_ids`. The first UI this value has ever had.

**D2. Webhook status** — whether an `ItemAdded` has been received, when the last one was, and whether
the delta poll is carrying the load. Proposal 106 (`spec-v2.2-proposals.md:1281`) sketches exactly
that. Do **not** invent a mode flag nobody sets; report both facts and let the operator read which
path is working.

**D3. Delete the placeholder at `:326`** and update the page docstring at `:5-15`, which currently
describes a page that no longer exists.

**D4. The refused-match list.** `:308` says M5's pipeline consumes `ResolveReport.unmatched`; once
M5.2 widens it, this card can show what the household has that the app could not identify. Small,
and it is the operator's only view of stage 1's parks.

### Phase E — System's remainder

**E1. `/api/admin/system` gains queue depth and last syncs.** Queue depth reads M5.1's
`acquire.queue.stats()`; last syncs read the existing `job_run` rows. Keep the route's residue
discipline: the queries live in the domain packages, not in `api/admin.py`
(`test_layering_guards.py:421-434`, where `admin.py: 17` is already the largest entry).

**E2. Logs.** §6.6's fifth item. The honest minimum is the last N lines of the app's own log with a
level filter; anything more is a log viewer nobody asked for. If the owner would rather defer it,
say so here and narrow §6.6 rather than shipping an empty panel.

**E3. This is the budgeted red diff.** `e2e/specs/18-system.spec.js:42` and `:87` assert the key set
is exactly `['backup','jobs','secrets']`. Adding a fourth key fails it **by design**; update the
spec's `FACTS` and its comment in the same commit, and do not add a waiver.

**E4. `admin/system/+page.svelte:86-91`'s copy** currently ends *"queue depth, last syncs and logs
are §6.6's too and arrive with M5"*. That sentence becomes false; replace it.

**E5. Read-only stays read-only.** `api/admin.py:810-812` argues it: rotation and repair are
`spielplan-secrets`, the dump is the worker's, *"a card that could start either would be the M5
surface this one is deliberately not"*. Queue depth and logs are reads; **do not add a "drain the
queue" button** — that is an acquisition action and it belongs on M5.6's board if it belongs
anywhere.

### Phase F — the harness and the guards

**F1. `ops/devstub.py`** gains the new routes; `test_devstub_contract.py:64` compares its path set to
the app's.

**F2. `ALLOWED_RESIDUE`** — if `api/admin.py` gains raw SQL, its entry (17) and `RESIDUE_TOTAL` (60)
move together, and the guard checks both directions. Prefer moving the query into a domain package:
`api/artifacts.py`'s entry of 6 is the precedent M4.14 set.

**F3. e2e numbering**, agreed with M5.6. Filename-ordered, single-worker, stateful; number into the
sequence and do not renumber anything.

## 7. Exit criterion, as something to run

> On a real install: enabling an extraction provider returns its **per-title cost estimate and the
> projected monthly figure against the remaining cap before the setting is persisted** — and
> cancelling leaves the stored configuration byte-identical. The estimate names the model and the
> price basis it used, and refuses to print a number for a model whose price is unknown. Changing the
> pass count recomputes both that figure and the flywheel's queued estimates. With the meter at the
> cap, an admin retry of a parked stage-6 job is refused with that reason shown rather than queued.
> The Jellyfin card lists the server's libraries and persists a pick that the delta poll then honours,
> and reports when the last `ItemAdded` arrived. The System card reports queue depth by state, each
> connector's last sync and the recent log lines beside the three facts M4.7 shipped, with no control
> anywhere on it that starts a job, drains a queue or rotates a key.

| # | measure | pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | enable a provider, read the response | estimate + projected monthly + remaining cap, all present |
| 2 | the stored config after that call | unchanged |
| 3 | confirm | stored; meter unchanged (no call made) |
| 4 | change the pass count | the figure changes proportionally; the flywheel's total does too |
| 5 | estimate for an unknown model | "unknown", not a number |
| 6 | estimate's caption | names the model and the price basis |
| 7 | meter at the cap, admin retry | refused with `over spend cap`; nothing queued |
| 8 | empty key field on save | stored key retained |
| 9 | stored key rendered anywhere | never |
| 10 | library pick | persists; the next delta poll reads only the picked libraries |
| 11 | webhook status | names the last `ItemAdded` received |
| 12 | `GET /api/admin/system` keys | five groups; `e2e/specs/18-system.spec.js`'s `FACTS` updated in the same commit |
| 13 | any control on System that writes | none |
| 14 | both pages on the phone | every control >= 48 px |

Checks 1-3 are the milestone's thesis in three lines: the number comes first, and saying no costs
nothing.

## 9. Risks and gotchas

**"Before enabling" is a sequencing requirement and it is easy to satisfy in the wrong order.** An
optimistic save plus a rollback shows the number *after* the setting persisted, and the coverage
row's `what` is about the ordering. Make it an explicit preview call and assert the stored config is
unchanged.

**The estimate that reassures and the estimate that protects are different numbers.** §6.6's
per-title figure is small enough to be ignored; the flywheel's batch total is the one that can be
four figures. Both live on these two pages and they must not be drawn with the same weight.

**A price table hard-coded today is wrong on a known date.** gemini-3.7-flash doubles on 2027-01-01
(`mdc/config.py:157-159`), and the whole 2.5 family already 404s for new keys (`:151-153`). Decision
343 exists for this.

**`spec_coverage.toml:3159` forbids a test on parallel mode's default.** Do not add one to close a
red row; take decision 324.

**`e2e/specs/18-system.spec.js:87` fails the moment System grows a fourth key.** That is the guard
working (`:82-84` says so). Update `FACTS`, its comment and the coverage row in one commit; do not
waive it.

**Keys are write-only and the page can only tell you *whether* there is one.** The Jellyfin card's
idiom (`admin/connectors/+page.svelte:10-12`) exists because §14.3 makes a stored credential
admin-equivalent. Three more providers is three more chances to render a secret.

**A Gemini key must never be in a URL.** M5.5's guard covers the backend; on this page the risk is a
test fixture or a debug log. `push/send.py:92-107` shows how easily the full URL reaches a log line.

**`test_static_contracts.py:1041` fails when a connector seed variable is not forwarded by compose.**
Adding three provider variables without touching `docker-compose.yml` reddens the build, by design.

**`ALLOWED_RESIDUE` moves in both directions.** `api/admin.py` already carries 17 raw statements, the
