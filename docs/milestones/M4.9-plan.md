# M4.9 — from the corpus to the card: loaders, read layer, Home

**This milestone is not in §12**, and it is not the second half of M4.5. M4.5 made the real
bundle *importable*; it never asked what the rows look like once they are in, and nothing did.
The corpus ships two department spellings for the same job, two facet namings for the same
vocabulary, and a term id that already carries its own facet — and the chain that reads those
rows was written against a fixture in which none of that is true. The result is one bug with
four faces: the loader stores both vocabularies, the read layer emits the double-qualified
facet, the chip prints it twice and loses all eleven §6.8 colours, and the each-block is keyed
on the term alone. The last link of the same chain is a rank-2 critical: **the title card
throws inside the client render on 1,216 of 19,071 real titles**, because `credits_for`'s
read-time dedupe returns two rows under one client key. This milestone makes the app read the
rows the real export actually ships.

Depends on **M4.8**: the fixture must carry a duplicate department spelling and both facet
namings before any assertion here can fail for the right reason. Against today's
`make_bundle.py` — which writes `characters.morally_grey` under facet `characters`
(`backend/tests/fixtures/make_bundle.py:96`) and no duplicate department — every test below
passes vacuously. That is M4.5's own lesson, and it is the reason this milestone has a
dependency rather than a fixture step of its own.

## 1. Why these changes are one milestone

The chain is `importer/{load,dna,reviews,meta}.py` → `db/library.py` → `home/{shelves,why,rail}.py`
→ `TitleDetail` / `ShelfRow` / `PosterCard`. It is one data shape, and each layer currently
guesses it differently.

Take the facet. The corpus stores the vocabulary id as `characters.amateur_sleuth` and the
extraction axis as `character_dynamics` (measured, read-only, on
`v20260828/content.sqlite`: `dna_tag` facets are `narrative_themes` 7,521 · `mood_tone` 4,529 ·
`plot_structure` 3,287 · `visual_style` 2,972 · `character_dynamics` 2,875 · `sensibility`
2,352 · `setting_place` 2,067 · `sound_score` 1,835 · `pacing_energy` 1,671 ·
`register_audience` 1,527 · `setting_era` 904). `importer/dna.py:87` derives `dna_facet` from
the term prefix; `importer/dna.py:303-305` and `:371-373` copy the shipped facet column
verbatim. So **29,188 of 31,540 `dna_tag` rows and 206,151 of 223,136 `dna_projected` rows hold
a facet that does not join `dna_facet`** — and every one of the eleven distinct term prefixes in
the shipped data (`characters, era, mood, pacing, place, register, sensibility, sound,
structure, themes, visual`) is exactly the vocabulary's own facet id, so the loader already
knows the right answer twenty lines away from where it writes the wrong one.

Fixing that in the loader alone leaves the client printing `mood.mood.hopeful`, because
`TitleDetail.svelte:221` renders `{tag.facet}.{tag.term}` and the term already carries the
prefix. Fixing the client alone leaves every chip grey, because `facetColour` looks up
`mood_tone`. Fixing both without the migration leaves the already-seeded install wrong for
ever, because decision 162 seeds content **once** — there is no re-import that would repair it.
And fixing all three without renaming `--facet-character` to `--facet-characters` leaves one of
eleven facets neutral anyway, because the shipped vocabulary file is `vocab_characters_v1.tsv`
and the palette is spelled singular in seven places.

The credit key is the same argument at the other end of the same SELECT. `db/library.py:331`
groups by `(person_id, name, department, job)`; TMDB records leads under both `Acting` and
`Actor` (and `Writing`/`Writer`, `Editing`/`Editor`, `Directing`/`Director` — eighteen
department spellings in the shipped `credit` table), so one person with one job comes back as
two rows. `TitleDetail.svelte:178` keys them on `c.person_id + c.job`. Svelte 5 calls
`e.each_key_duplicate` in the **production** branch too, so this throws inside the render.
Measured: **7,918 (title, person, job) triples span more than one department, across 1,216 of
19,071 titles**. The repair edits both ends of the chain — the `GROUP BY` and the client key —
which is why `schema-credits-character-follows-heap-order` (the unordered `array_agg` in the
same SELECT) and `cs-23` (the silent slice to twelve, over a median of 24 credits) are here
too. Four findings rewriting one query with incompatible aggregates is one diff or four
conflicting ones.

Home lives here rather than with Rank because `home/shelves.py` reads `db/library.py`'s shapes
far more than it reads `rank/board.py`: the shelf card is `CARD_SELECT` over the same spine,
the why-line chips are the same facet, and the cold badge is decided by the same
`title_prior` join the catalog already carries.

**Editorial licence, stated so no other milestone claims it.** The `characters` rename is a
seven-file atomic change. This milestone alone may edit `frontend/src/lib/design.css`'s facet
palette block (`:52-62`) and `backend/tests/test_static_contracts.py`'s `VOCAB_V1_FACETS`
(`:22-25`) in that one commit. M4.7 and M4.15 must not hold those lines.

## 2. What is broken today

Every file:line below was read at the m45 head (`50753a3` plus the uncommitted spec
amendments). Every corpus number was re-measured read-only against
`movie_data_curator/data/export_bundle/v20260828/content.sqlite`.

### A. The facet vocabulary — four faces of one bug

**1. The loader keeps two vocabularies.**
[`imp-facet-naming-loader-keeps-two-vocabularies`, `schema-dna-term-facet-double-qualified`]
`importer/dna.py:87` computes `facet = term.split(".", 1)[0]` for `dna_vocabulary`/`dna_term`;
`importer/dna.py:303-305` (`load_tags`) and `:371-373` (`load_projected`) select
`term, facet` straight out of sqlite and insert both verbatim. `dna_tag.facet` therefore never
equals `dna_facet.facet` — 29,188 / 31,540 and 206,151 / 223,136 rows mismatch — and there is no
FK between them (`0004_dna.sql:73-88`) to notice. Every per-facet aggregate that joins the two
returns nothing. **Should:** the app's facet is the vocabulary's facet id, i.e. the term prefix;
the corpus's extraction label survives as a §10 report line, not as data.

**2. The read layer builds a facet that already exists.**
[`schema-dna-term-facet-double-qualified`] `db/library.py:113` and `:199` both test
`lower(dt.term) = $n OR lower(dt.facet || '.' || dt.term) = $n`, and the comment above them
(`:108-113`) asserts *"a `dna_tag` row holds the bare term and its facet in separate columns, so
`mood.cosy` exists only at query time."* That sentence is false of the shipped data: the term
**is** `mood.cosy`. So `?dna=dread` matches nothing, `?dna=mood.dread` matches, and
`?dna=mood_tone.mood.dread` matches too. **Should:** accept the bare term or the qualified id
(`lower(dt.term) = $n OR split_part(lower(dt.term), '.', 2) = $n`), and the comment must be
rewritten rather than left standing.

**3. The chip prints the facet twice and the palette is dead.**
[`dd-fe-dna-facet-rendered-twice-and-palette-is-dead`] `TitleDetail.svelte:221` renders
`{tag.facet}.{tag.term}`, `:241` renders `{p.facet}.{p.term}`, `ShelfRow.svelte:109` renders
`{t.facet}.{t.term}`. On real data those read `narrative_themes.themes.love_romance`. The
colour comes from `facetColour` over an eleven-name set (`TitleDetail.svelte:27-33`,
`home.svelte.js:18-27`) whose names are the *term prefixes*, so 92.5% of tags fall through to
`var(--ink-4)`. §6.8 calls the facet colour identity, not decoration; it is not in effect
anywhere. **Should:** render `{term}` alone — it is already qualified — and colour by the
normalised facet.

**4. One of the eleven facets is spelled wrong in seven places.**
[`imp-facet-naming-loader-keeps-two-vocabularies`] The shipped vocabulary directory holds
`vocab_characters_v1.tsv` and every shipped term prefix is `characters`; the palette says
`character` at `design.css:58`, `TitleDetail.svelte:29`, `home.svelte.js:20`,
`home.test.js:257`, `ModelRail.svelte:207`, `importer/dna.py:31` and
`test_static_contracts.py:24` — the guard pins the wrong spelling, so the guard is what would
reject the fix.

### B. The title card's credits — one SELECT, three findings

**5. Duplicate credit keys throw on 1,216 real titles.** *(rank-2 critical)*
[`dd-fe-title-card-duplicate-credit-keys-throws`] `db/library.py:314-336` collapses to one row
per `(person, department, job)` — its docstring says "dedupe at read time", §4.1 —
and `TitleDetail.svelte:178` keys `(c.person_id + c.job)`. 7,918 triples across 1,216 titles
have two departments; 816 of them fall inside the first twelve credits the card renders. Svelte
5's keyed each throws in dev **and** production; with no `+error.svelte` the panel breaks
mid-render and platform scores, both DNA tiers and the model line never draw. **Should:** one
`(person, job)` is one row, and the client key is total and delimited.

**6. The character shown depends on COPY order.**
[`schema-credits-character-follows-heap-order`] `db/library.py:327` takes
`(array_agg(c.character) FILTER (...))[1]` with no `ORDER BY`, while every other aggregate in
the same query is `min()` or `array_agg(DISTINCT …)`. 2,300 `(title, person)` pairs carry more
than one distinct non-NULL character across sources. **Should:** deterministic and
source-stable.

**7. The card truncates to twelve with no count and no expansion.**
[`cs-23-title-card-truncates-credits-silently`] `TitleDetail.svelte:178` slices to twelve on a
corpus whose median is 24 credits per title (p75 29, max 1,535) — 89.7% of titles carry more
than twelve. There is no count line, no disclosure, and no caller anywhere in `frontend/src`
for `GET /api/people/{person_id}`, whose docstring (`api/library.py:200`) quotes §6.0's
"each person tappable → filters the library to their filmography". §6.0 applies a count-line
discipline to the kind toggle and this surface ignores it. **Should:** twelve stays the
collapsed default, with `12 of 47` and a disclosure over `data.credits`, which the client
already holds.

### C. Keys and scoping in the DNA read path

**8. Both DNA each-blocks are keyed on the term alone.**
[`fec07-dna-each-blocks-keyed-on-term-alone`] `TitleDetail.svelte:218` and `:239` key on
`tag.term` / `p.term` while `dna_tag`'s uniqueness is `(title_id, version, term, provider)`
(`0004_dna.sql:83`) — §6.6's parallel extraction mode is exactly the case that produces two rows
with one term. Twenty lines above, the platform-scores block carries a comment recording that
this same crash already shipped once and was fixed by keying on `platform + ':' + metric`.

**9. `dna_tag`'s UNIQUE never fires.** [`schema-dna-tag-unique-never-fires`] `provider` is NULL
for every corpus row and NULLs are distinct in a UNIQUE index, so the arbiter never fires and
the importer works around it with DELETE-by-version + INSERT (`importer/dna.py:308-317`, whose
comment states the workaround). Two identical rows insert cleanly and the card renders the term
twice. **Should:** `provider` NOT NULL DEFAULT `''`, so the index the schema declares is the
index that exists.

**10. Three DNA readers ignore the vocabulary version.**
[`schema-dna-tag-arbiter-and-unscoped-version-reads`] `db/library.py:352-360` (`dna_for`),
`:110-115` (the `dna` predicate) and `api/library.py:154-171` (`similar_by_term`'s two neighbour
queries) carry no `version` predicate, while `home/why.py:81-89` deliberately scopes to the
newest `dna_vocabulary` row and says why: *"a household that has imported two bundles has two
versions and the shelves must not mix them."* Latent under decision 163, live the day a
vocabulary migration lands.

### D. Catalogue and route hygiene

**11. Pagination is not a total order.** [`schema-list-titles-pagination-not-a-total-order`]
`db/library.py:255` orders by `t.year DESC NULLS LAST, lower(t.name)` with no tiebreaker and
pages with LIMIT/OFFSET while `+page.svelte:114-121` appends. Ties reorder both when Postgres
switches sort method (top-N heapsort at low offsets, quicksort at high ones) and whenever a row
is rewritten between pages. Reproduced twice by the review: 600 tied titles → 3 duplicated, 3
missing; 300 tied rows with an UPDATE between pages → 61 and 61. The corpus has **584 tie
groups covering 1,175 titles, plus 340 NULL-year titles that all tie on the first key**. The two
other OFFSET readers already tiebreak (`scoring/serve.py:330`, `ledger/refit.py:1090`).

**12. LIKE metacharacters are not escaped, in two copies.**
[`schema-search-q-like-metacharacters-unescaped`] `db/library.py:72` and `scoring/serve.py:245`
both build `%{q.lower()}%`. `q='%'` returns everything, `q='_'` matches any one-character name,
`q='h_at'` returns *Heat*, and the hidden-by-kind count is computed from the same predicate so
it is wrong in the same way. Bound as a parameter, so this is not injection — the wildcards
simply keep their meaning.

**13. Two authenticated listing routes ignore the kind partition.**
[`cs-57-similar-by-term-and-filmography-do-not-partition-by-kind`]
`api/library.py:146-177` selects `t.kind` into the SELECT list and the GROUP BY and into no
predicate; `api/library.py:199-215`'s filmography has no kind predicate at all. §4.1 rule 5
requires every ranking surface to partition by kind, and the landmine guard
(`test_landmine_guards.py:161-186`) only reads `db/library.py`'s builder, so neither route is
visible to it.

**14. `similar-by-term`'s limit is unvalidated.** [`schema-similar-by-term-limit-unbounded`,
`rrh-lifecycle-08-similar-by-term-limit-unvalidated` — two reviewers, one defect]
`api/library.py:147` declares `limit: int = 12` and passes it into `LIMIT $2`; `-1` is a 500
through the `PostgresError` handler, `100000` is a 200 over the whole join. The sibling catalog
route two dozen lines up is `Query(60, ge=1, le=200)`. The two findings propose `le=60` and
`le=100`; take **`le=60`**, matching the finding that also names the route test to extend.

### E. Home

**20. Projected DNA weight outranks the extracted tier.**
[`tq2-projected-dna-weight-outranks-the-extracted-tier`] `importer/dna.py:363` stores

## 8. What this milestone deliberately does NOT do

- **It does not build the fixture.** M4.8 owns `make_bundle.py`'s duplicate department spelling
  and both facet namings. Everything here is asserted against that fixture and, where
  `CORPUS_BUNDLE_DIR` is set, against the artifact.
- **It does not touch the Cold Tower, the feature contract or placement.** M4.5 fixed the nine
  block grammars; `n_companies` becomes non-zero here only as a consequence of loading
  `title_company`, and no coordinate is recomputed.
- **It does not apply the corrections ledger.** §8 stage 3 is M5's derive; this milestone only
  stops the import report claiming otherwise.
- **It does not build the Map or author the axis TSVs.** §6.4 is M6; cs-22 removes the copy that
  promised the Map rather than making it true, and cs-59 becomes a Data-card task and an owner
  decision, not an authoring project.
- **It does not repair the 73 mojibake rows.** The knowledge is upstream: the corpus exporter
  writing repaired bodies, or a shipped `review_fixes_v1.tsv` applied by `review.id`. This
  milestone turns a silent note into a warning and stops the coverage row asserting a repair
  that never happens.
- **It does not migrate the DNA vocabulary.** Decision 163 stands: a vocabulary change is a
  migration with its own plan. The version-scoping in step 4 is what makes that deferral safe
  rather than silent.
- **It does not add `episode_count`/`season_count`, a `/people/{id}` page, keyset pagination, an

## 9. Risks and gotchas

**Migrations.** `backend/migrations/NNNN_*.sql` are sha256-checksummed and a mismatch is a hard
startup error. Write 0016 once, in full; anything discovered later is 0017. `0004_dna.sql`,
`0003_content.sql` and `0015_seed.sql` are all applied — do not touch them. Coordinate the
number with M4.6.

**The `GROUP BY` change breaks the `ORDER BY`.** Dropping `c.department` from
`db/library.py:331` makes `ORDER BY (c.department = 'Directing') DESC` at `:332` invalid;
Postgres rejects the query outright rather than mis-sorting. It becomes
`bool_or(c.department = 'Directing') DESC`. Related and **not** in scope: the corpus has
eighteen department spellings including a bare `Director` beside `Directing`, so a person
credited only as `Director` still sorts below the cast. Do not normalise departments silently —
raise it as a finding if it matters.

**Half the facet fix is worse than none.** The loader change alone gives `mood.mood.hopeful` a
correct colour; the client change alone gives the right label in grey. Land steps 1, 2 and the
palette rename in one commit, and remember that the backfill `UPDATE` is what makes it true on
the install M4.5 already seeded — decision 162 means there is no re-import.

**Arming `provider` NOT NULL arms the UNIQUE.** Verified: the shipped bundle has zero duplicate
`(title_id, term)` groups in `dna_tag` and zero in `dna_projected`, so the seed still imports.
Re-verify after M4.8 lands — the fixture must gain a duplicate *department* and both facet
namings, not a duplicate `dna_tag` row, or the import will start failing for a reason nobody
intended.

**Changing the term-weight expression changes Tonight.** `dna_tagged` exposes
`dna_projected.weight AS confidence`, so the bounded projected branch moves the tilt vectors,
the authored-axis positions, `terms_carried_by` and the winner card's match lines as well as
Home's why-lines. The guard test must be written over both readers, and D2 must be answered
before the edit rather than after.

**A green no-DB run has not run the layers that matter here.** Integration tests skip silently
without `TEST_DATABASE_URL` (auto-loaded from `.env.test`) and the PGlite schema tests skip
without `backend/tests/pglite/node_modules`. Almost every assertion in §6 is integration. Bring
the database up with
`docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f ops/compose.dev.yml up -d db` — overlays are never
auto-loaded.

**Run pytest with the path.** `backend/.venv/Scripts/python -m pytest backend/tests -q`; never
bare `pytest` from the repo root, because the config with `asyncio_mode=auto` lives in
`backend/pyproject.toml` and async tests error without it.

**e2e is stateful and ordered.** Extend `04-title-card.spec.js` and `10-home.spec.js`; do not add
files or parallelise. Plain `playwright test` against a used stack makes first-boot and bundle
specs skip and fake-pass — `node e2e/run.mjs`'s two phases and backend restart are load-bearing.
`e2e/reset.mjs` drops the database and wipes `data/artifacts`.

**The static guard is what will reject the fix.** `test_static_contracts.py:22-25`'s
`VOCAB_V1_FACETS` pins `character`. Changing it is the licence claimed in §1; changing it
*without* the palette and both component sets is how one of eleven facets stays neutral while
the guard says everything is fine.

**`ops/devstub.py` mirrors `credits_for`** at `:513-521`. Keep it in step — but it is a harness,
not the app, and `backend/spielplan/api/` wins on any disagreement.

**Test doubles are refusers, not mocks.** `ops/fake_jellyfin.py` rejects the admin key on the
Played write on purpose. If the poster route (step 10) reaches Jellyfin's image endpoint in a
test, expect a refusal and assert the placeholder rather than "fixing" the double.

**Conventions.** Diffs are surgical — every changed line traces to a finding above; `ruff format`
is not enforced, so do not reformat wholesale; line length is 108 because spec sentences are
quoted inline and must not wrap; comments argue why and cite the clause; commits are
`feat(M4.9):` / `fix(M4.9):` with a lowercase subject and a body citing sections; and every
console or test string stays ASCII, because Windows cp1252 consoles crash on decorative glyphs.
